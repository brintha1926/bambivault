Designed for privacy

Clear safeguards govern how passwords and account information are processed.

Passwords submitted for assessment are not retained in plaintext.
Breach checks transmit only a partial hash, never the complete password.
Guest assessments are not linked to an account or retained in account history.
Vault data is encrypted using the account holder's master password and Account Key.
Administrative reporting contains aggregated and anonymised statistics only.
Account deletion removes the associated profile and vault data.

Frequently asked questions

Can I safely assess an active password? +

BambiVault does not retain submitted passwords in plaintext, and breach checks use only a partial hash. For additional caution, use a representative password with a comparable structure.

Do I need an account? +

No. Password assessments are available immediately. An account provides access to analysis history, personalised insights, and encrypted vault capabilities.

How does my account password differ from my vault master password? +

Your account password authenticates access to BambiVault. Your vault master password and Account Key are separate security factors used to decrypt vault entries.

Who can access institutional analytics? +

Access is limited to authorised administrators. Reports contain aggregated statistics and do not expose individual passwords or identifiable submissions.
