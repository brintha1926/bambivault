STAY SECURE — WHAT TO WATCH FOR
🎣
Phishing
Attackers send fake emails or messages mimicking a trusted source — your bank, university, or a colleague — to trick you into revealing your password directly. No breach database can catch this; the only defence is checking sender addresses carefully and never entering credentials through a link you didn't navigate to yourself.
🔁
Credential Stuffing
When one site is breached, attackers automatically try the same email+password combination across thousands of other sites. Reusing a password — even a strong one — means a single breach elsewhere can compromise every account where you reused it.
🔐
Password Managers
A password manager generates and stores a unique, high-entropy password for every account, so you only need to remember one master password. This removes the two biggest human risk factors this dashboard measures: reuse and predictable patterns.
